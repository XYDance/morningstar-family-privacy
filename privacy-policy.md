# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: May 2, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how MorningStar Family ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares your personal information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

- **Names** — when you create a family profile
- **Location data** — to show local weather for each family member
- **Photos** — taken during morning tasks
- **Audio recordings** — from the singing task
- **Push notification tokens** — to send completion notifications to parents

## How We Use Your Information

- To deliver the app's features and services
- To enable family members to see each other's activity and profiles
- To send parents a notification when the morning routine is complete

## AI Features

The app uses Anthropic's AI to check if a child's outfit matches the weather. Photos used for this check are processed by Anthropic and are not stored by us.

## Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. To delete your account and data, email us at xy@xinyingdance.com — we will remove all data within 30 days.
